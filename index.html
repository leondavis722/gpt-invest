FAQ

Questions & answers

How does it actually make money?

It reads faster and wider than anyone in the market. When a stock's price and the facts in its filings drift apart, that gap is an opportunity — the AI takes the position, sets the exit before entry, and closes it when the gap closes. Thousands of small, reasoned edges instead of one big bet.

How fast can I withdraw my money?

Any time you want. Request a withdrawal in the app and the AI closes the positions it needs to — your funds land back in your account within one to three business days. No lock-up period, no exit fee, no phone call to talk you out of it. It's your money; it just happens to have an analyst working on it.

Is it free? Why the $250?

Applying and watching the AI work costs nothing during the research preview. The $250 minimum deposit isn't a fee — it's your capital, the money the AI actually invests for you. It stays yours, and you can withdraw it any time.

Why does a specialist call me before I can join?

Because automated investing isn't right for everyone, and we'd rather find that out before your money is involved. The specialist checks your goals, experience and risk tolerance — a ten-minute suitability call. It also keeps the beta small enough for the AI to serve every account well. Not everyone is approved.

How is this different from a trading bot?

Bots follow price patterns and can't tell you why. GPT Invest reads the underlying reality — filings, calls, footnotes — and every trade it makes comes with the reason and the source in writing. When you can read the why, you can trust the what.
