Why use our tech

Three things no dashboard does.

Built to make you understand your portfolio — not to make you trade it faster.

It reads what you won't

A 300-page annual report, a 90-minute earnings call, a footnote on page 214. Summarised in seconds — with a link to the exact line it came from.

It shows your real risk

Concentration, overlap between funds, currency exposure, fee drag. The numbers that decide your outcome, surfaced before the ones that flatter it.

It can't touch your money

Read-only by design. No order path exists in the system, no funds are held, and no deposit is ever requested. It reads and explains. That's all.

FAQ

Questions & answers

Is it free?

In the concept, yes — free during research preview, with no card and no deposit. There is no funding step anywhere in the product, because it never handles money.

How long before I can start?

Adding holdings takes about two minutes, and the first answer comes back in seconds. There's no onboarding call and no waiting on an account manager.

Will it tell me what to buy?

No. It explains what you already own and what the filings say. It doesn't recommend trades, forecast prices, or promise returns — no tool honestly can, and any product claiming otherwise is worth walking away from.

Can it be wrong?

Yes. Language models make mistakes, which is why every figure carries a link to its source and low-confidence answers say so up front instead of guessing with confidence.

Is this a real product?

No — it's a design concept built as a portfolio exercise, with no company, backend, or affiliation behind it. The visual language references openai.com; the name, logo and product idea are original.
